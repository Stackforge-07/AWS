============================================================
1. OFFICIAL SIH26073 INPUT SCOPE — STRICT REQUIREMENT
============================================================

This project is for:

SIH 2026
Problem Statement: SIH26073
Organization: Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
Department: India Meteorological Department

The anomaly-detection intelligence MUST strictly follow the
official problem statement.

The ONLY meteorological parameters allowed as model inputs are:

1. Temperature (°C)
2. Atmospheric Pressure (hPa)
3. Relative Humidity (%)

These three variables form the complete meteorological input space
for the anomaly-detection system.

DO NOT require or use any additional environmental/weather variable
for anomaly classification.

Specifically, DO NOT use:

- wind speed
- wind direction
- rainfall
- precipitation
- solar radiation
- visibility
- cloud cover
- weather-code labels
- satellite observations
- radar observations
- lightning observations
- soil variables
- air-quality variables
- external weather forecasts
- Numerical Weather Prediction variables
- external forecast temperatures
- external forecast pressure
- external forecast humidity
- or any other meteorological parameter outside T/P/RH.

The platform must demonstrate that meaningful anomaly detection,
sensor-fault identification, temporal reasoning, multivariate
consistency analysis, spatial validation and sensor-health monitoring
can be achieved from:

Temperature
+
Atmospheric Pressure
+
Relative Humidity

alone.

order, transmit and manage measurements.

station_id

model/firmware version

These are NOT meteorological predictors.

They must not be treated as additional environmental variables.

Timestamp may be used to:

- maintain correct sequence
- calculate elapsed time
- build past-only temporal features
- learn seasonal/time-of-day patterns

Station identity may be used to:

- maintain station-specific history
- isolate station models/state
- perform comparison with other AWS observations

Communication metadata may be used only for:

- missing-data detection
- packet-loss detection
- stale-data detection
- communication-failure classification

============================================================
1.3 CORE SCIENTIFIC CHALLENGE
============================================================

The system must answer:

"Given only Temperature, Atmospheric Pressure and Relative Humidity,
is this observation consistent with normal behaviour, genuine weather,
or a sensor/data anomaly?"

This is the central problem.

Do not solve a broader weather-forecasting problem.

Do not turn the system into a multi-source weather platform.

Stay tightly aligned with SIH26073.

============================================================
2. ALLOWED PHYSICS-DERIVED FEATURES
============================================================

Physics-derived quantities ARE allowed because they are mathematically
computed from the three official variables rather than obtained from
additional sensors.

They must be clearly labelled:

DERIVED FROM T/P/RH

They are not independent observations.

Allowed examples include:

From Temperature + Relative Humidity:

- saturation vapour pressure
- actual vapour pressure
- dew point
- dew-point depression
- vapour pressure deficit

From Temperature + Pressure + Relative Humidity:

- mixing ratio
- specific humidity
- virtual temperature
- approximate air density

From Temperature + Pressure:

- potential-temperature-like thermodynamic transforms
  where scientifically appropriate

From repeated Pressure observations:

- pressure tendency
- pressure rate of change
- pressure acceleration

From repeated Temperature observations:

- temperature rate of change
- temperature acceleration

From repeated RH observations:

- humidity rate of change

These features provide additional REPRESENTATIONS of the official
T/P/RH measurements.

They must never be described as additional independent sensors.

============================================================
15. CENTRAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE
============================================================

The project should repeatedly demonstrate this statement:

"SkyGuard solves SIH26073 using only the three meteorological
parameters specified in the official problem statement:
Temperature, Atmospheric Pressure and Relative Humidity."

All higher-level intelligence is derived from these three streams.

Temporal intelligence comes from their histories.

Seasonal intelligence comes from their historical patterns.

Physics intelligence comes from mathematical relationships among them.

Multivariate intelligence comes from their joint behaviour.

Spatial intelligence comes from comparing the same three parameters
across AWS stations.

TinyML learns normal sequences of these three parameters.

The Virtual Weather Twin estimates their expected values.

The fusion model combines evidence derived from these three parameters.

This strict input discipline must be maintained throughout the
entire architecture.
