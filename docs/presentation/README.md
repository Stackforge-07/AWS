# SkyGuard SIH presentation

The six-slide SIH 2026 presentation is maintained in Canva:

<https://www.canva.com/design/DAHWNHadKp8/_Y_-ND4KDp9A0J7cToBVNQ/edit>

The committed deck follows the SIH idea structure: title, proposed solution, technical approach, feasibility and viability, impact and benefits, and research/references. Registration-specific values remain placeholders in Canva until the official team name, team ID, theme and category are available.

The local PNGs are review previews. `make_visuals.py` regenerates the original pipeline and fault-recall visuals used in the deck. The deck uses the measured v2 comparison from `ml/artifacts/evaluation_v2_comparison.json`; those numbers are synthetic development results and are labeled that way in the slides.

Research references used for structure and presentation guidance:

- [SIH 2024 official guidelines](https://sih.gov.in/letters/Guidelines-College-SPOC.pdf)
- [SIH 2024 idea template](https://www.cmrit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIH2024_IDEA_Presentation_Format.pdf)
- [Public past-deck collection](https://github.com/DgrnBoi/SIH-Past-PPTS-2024)
